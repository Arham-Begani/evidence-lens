# Publication boundaries and rights

This public copy contains AI-assisted code, original visual elements and controlled source-card paraphrases prepared before the December 4–6, 2026 sprint. It is not a qualifying event-weekend result or a completed empirical paper.

The 16 model response objects, 35 claims, source packets and exact recorded input/output files are preserved. `data/saved-development.json` is an explicitly derived public view: operational timestamps, token accounting and recovery diagnostics are excluded, with the retained/omitted fields listed in `site/publication.json`. The original validation records and archives remain unchanged locally. The public audit and all-answers report describe the export; they do not impersonate the private process audit.

No personal author email, affiliation or guardian assertion is included. Submission identity files, raw conversations, operational logs, CLI event streams and machine-specific screenshots are excluded. Blank review forms stay blank. Historical archive compatibility and private review-packet tests are not included in this public test subset; this does not change the original internal results.

The sample cards are short, researcher-authored factual paraphrases with source links and scope qualifiers. They are not verbatim articles, independent observations or copies of full published reports. The nine draft source families remain author-known; none is a held-out result. Sources include MITRE, Microsoft, Google/Mandiant, UK NCSC, Singapore CSA, Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 and ESET, with original URLs retained in the datasets. No original report PDFs, articles, vendor logos or fonts are redistributed. Third-party source material remains with its respective rightsholders; no endorsement is implied.
