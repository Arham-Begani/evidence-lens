What is real here?+

These are 16 captured Astra development responses containing 35 final claims. They use one JumbledPath case, four prompting methods and four evidence conditions. The cards are controlled researcher-written paraphrases, not full published reports. Changing a selection loads an existing answer; it does not run a model.

What has been checked?+

The original internal validation checked saved answers against recorded inputs and event streams. This public copy preserves the exact input and answer files; private process logs are excluded. All 16 meet the schema and final-word limit, and their citation IDs resolve. An internal AI-assisted review exists. Independent source and semantic review remain pending. Citation validity is not a factual-support score.

What can’t this case establish?+

One author-known case, one document order and short passages with explicit source cues cannot establish a winning method, generalization or calibrated confidence. The CLI wrapper and unavailable immutable model digest limit generation reproducibility. The preserved outputs themselves remain inspectable.
